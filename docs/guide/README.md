# kimchi user guide

How to make videos in the kimchi window. For driving kimchi from scripts and AI tools, see
[Controlling kimchi from AI and scripts](../AI_CONTROL.md).

1. [Getting started](getting-started.md): install, and a first cut from import to export.
2. [The window](window.md): home, the editor's panels, menus, the command palette, small windows,
   light and dark.
3. [Editing](editing.md): media, titles, tracks and clips, selecting, trimming, splitting, the
   inspector, speed and freeze frames, undo.
4. [Transitions and colour](transitions-and-colour.md): transitions on cuts, looks, corrections,
   green screen and LUTs.
5. [Captions](captions.md): transcribing speech on your computer, subtitle files, styling, and
   captions in the export.
6. [Animation, motion graphics and 3D](motion.md): keyframes, presets, templates, the Studio,
   modelling, rendering ahead and the 3D engines.
7. [Sound](sound.md): a clip's sound, the mixer, effects and plugins, loudness, beats, voice-overs
   and ryolune.
8. [Generation](generation.md): connecting models, the Generate panel, generating in the edit,
   provenance, ComfyUI workflows.
9. [The agent](agent.md): the Agent panel, its models, undoing a run, permissions.
10. [Export](export.md): formats, quality, hardware encoding, sound-only exports and stems.
11. [Settings and troubleshooting](settings.md): every settings page, updates, logs and crash
    reports, common problems.

[Keyboard shortcuts](shortcuts.md) lists the shortcuts; the keys used while moving things in the
Studio are in [its chapter](motion.md#moving-rotating-and-scaling).

On macOS the guide writes the keys as ⌘ (Command), ⌥ (Option) and ⇧ (Shift). On Windows and Linux,
⌘ is Ctrl and ⌥ is Alt.
