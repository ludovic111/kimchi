# Logos

These logos are the trademarks of their owners. kimchi uses them only to identify their services
and apps next to their names; it isn't endorsed by or affiliated with them.

Each file was downloaded on 2026-10-05 (the editors' and plugin formats' on 2026-10-06) from the owner's own site (or, where noted, the lsuite app's
own repository), then only resized to 128 × 128 px with ImageMagick (transparency kept; SVG files were
rasterised with resvg, transparent margins trimmed). Nothing was redrawn. `ui::logos::LOGOS` maps ids
to these files.
